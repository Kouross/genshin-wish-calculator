# Genshin Wish Probability Calculator

hi, im a french independent worker. hope u will understand my future me and work.

this is a small calculator i made to help with genshin wishes. hope u will enjoy it.

## how to use the calc

just put the information its asking for: your pity, your guarantee, your primogems, wishes and starglitter. choose what character constellation and weapon refinement you want, and what you already have.

you can choose to calculate for the character, the weapon, or both. if you choose both, the result is the chance to get the character to the constellation you want **and** the weapon to the refinement you want with your wishes.

for example, if you want varesa c2 and vesna c1, you need to calculate them separately, because the calc only takes one character goal at a time. if you dont already have the character, c2 means getting 3 copies, and c1 means getting 2 copies. the character name is only there to show your goal; it does not change the odds. the constellation you choose and what you already own are what matters.

160 primogems count as 1 wish, and every 5 starglitter count as 1 more wish. the calculator uses the number of wishes you already have too.

you can switch the website between french and english with the language button. after calculating, the wheel can spin to show a simulated result. it is only a simulation, so the real result in game can be different.

## files

keep `index.html` and `calculator.js` in the same folder. both files are needed for the calculator to work.

## small note

the result is an estimate, not a promise. soft pity and capturing radiance are modeled with estimates, and the calculator only models the inputs shown on the page. good luck with your wishes ✦
